is unevaluable on a backward spine (`2ef8e5d`; `AMEND_f4` §1). **F-18's eligible set is therefore

leg is re-declared as **"event-free at a measured miss rate of X%"**, and X travels with the
verdict.

this book; it is a material term of the hypothesis, and the declaration should say so.
