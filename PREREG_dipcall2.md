## 0. THE SIX CHANGES FROM `PREREG_dipcall.md`, every one stated before any outcome exists

### C1 — THE ARMS: THE NO-NEWS TIER ARMS ONLY, AT 5 AND 21 SESSIONS
Two arms, not four: **no-news × {5, 21 sessions}** on the **point-in-time $10B tier**, which

### C2 — THE NEWS ARM IS RECORDED `STRUCTURALLY UNREACHABLE`, NOT SILENTLY DROPPED
**This is the change that needs the most defending, so it is defended with measurements rather
than with an argument.**

DIP-CALL measured the news arm at **614 / 1,049** events (h21) and **616 / 1,053** (h5) against a
pre-committed 1,500 floor, out of **1,669** news events on the entire tier across eleven years.
Its free k-census then measured that **no threshold reaches the floor**: the smallest news cell is
**449 at k = 3.0, 619 at 2.5, 859 at 2.0 and 1,246 at k = 1.5** — and at k = 1.5 the median
event-day move is **−2.93pp**, so the "event" has stopped being a shock before the count arrives.
The structural reason is measured: **earnings reactions are 2.97% of all scoreable tier name-days
(18,985 of 638,880), and 3.29% of those on names with known coverage.** A share that fixed is not
moved by any choice of k.

**WHY DROPPING IT IS NOT CHOOSING THE DESIGN ON AN OUTCOME, AND THE DISTINCTION IS THE WHOLE
LICENCE FOR THIS REGISTER.** `MB1-SEL` holds that a control *"can only ever BLOCK a finding, never
produce one, so it adds no degree of freedom to any published claim"*, and `DC-1` is the precedent
for closing a design at a feasibility kill at zero trials. **DIP-CALL computed NO abnormal return,
NO option price and NO arm statistic of any kind** — its artifact records
`forward_return_touched: false` and `abnormal_return_touched: false`, and its arm script refused
by name and exited 1. **So nothing about which way the news arm would have gone was ever
observable.** The arm is dropped because the DATA cannot power it, which is a fact about the
panel, and not because of anything it said.

**AND THE RISK THAT REMAINS IS NAMED RATHER THAN WAVED PAST.** A successor register that re-runs a
*subset* of a predecessor's arms is a real second degree of freedom even when the narrowing is
feasibility-driven, because *choosing which subset* is a choice. **That is why `C4` charges trials
for it and why BH's `k` does NOT shrink.**

**VOID CONDITION (new): the news arm is UNTESTED, NOT NULL.** No statement about news-driven drops
may be drawn from this register in either direction. `A1`'s classifier still runs — it is how a
no-news event is *identified* — and `A1b`'s UNKNOWN names are still **counted and excluded by
name**, never folded into "no news".

### C4 — TRIALS: **2 EQUITY**, AND DIP-CALL'S 4 STAY CHARGED. **BH `k` STAYS 10.**
* **Step 1 = 2 EQUITY trials** (two arms), booked **before any runner exists**.
* **DIP-CALL's 4 equity trials are NOT refunded.** `E-1`'s precedent is explicit — it kept its
  trial for an arm withdrawn on a kill, because un-booking after seeing a kill fire is the shape
  this record warns against hardest, and `MA6` holds that **overstating `N` is the safe
  direction**. So the project pays **6** equity trials for 4 arms of which 2 are now scored. That
  overstates the search and is the direction that raises every gated claim.
* **Step 2 = 6 OPTIONS trials**, booked at the moment step 1's verdict authorises step 2 and
  **before any option price is read** (`A7`, carried over).
* **BH at q = 0.10 with `k` = 10, UNCHANGED.** The true batch here is 8 (2 + 6), and `k` = 8 would
  make the smallest threshold **0.0125** instead of **0.01000**. **Shrinking `k` after a
  predecessor's kill fired would make every surviving threshold EASIER, which is exactly what
  `W-28` forbids**, so the predecessor's declared `k` is kept. These two arms were members of
  DIP-CALL's pre-declared `k` = 10 batch and they carry that burden with them.

### C5 — 63 AND 126 SESSIONS STAY **NO-VERDICT**, AND THE REASON IS NOW A MEASURED ONE
DIP-CALL demoted them because *"power dies there"*, on the draft's **20pp** dispersion anchor.
**`A10` measured the real dispersion and the anchor was 1.71× too large: 11.667pp at h63.** So
`V6`'s banked **+0.5203pp** 63-day dip effect needs **8,848** events rather than ~26,001, against
**9,614** on the tier — **marginally reachable at 1.09×.**

**AND IT STILL CARRIES NO VERDICT, for a reason that is not about power at all: 9,614 is the
POOLED, UNSPLIT count, and `RESEARCH_CHARTER.md` §5's both-halves rule forbids an unsplit
verdict.** Split into two halves and residualised, the h63 cells fall far below 8,848, and split
further by arm they fall further. **So the honest statement is that the 63-session horizon is
reachable only in a form this project does not accept as a verdict** — and the correction to
DIP-CALL's stated reason is recorded rather than quietly inherited. Both horizons are computed and
reported as sensitivities. **Quoting either as a verdict is a void condition** (carried from
DIP-CALL §6.1).

## 1. WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT CHANGED, AND THE ONE PLACE THAT COSTS POWER

**`A6`'s TWO-SIDED *p* IS KEPT, AND A ONE-SIDED TEST IS AVAILABLE AND REFUSED.** DIP-CALL took
two-sided because its two news arms had **opposite** predicted signs (Chan 2003 reversal, Savor
2012 drift), so no uniform direction could be declared. **With the news arm gone that reason is
gone: Chan (2003) predicts no-news shocks REVERSE, i.e. POSITIVE, and a one-sided test is
genuinely licensed by the literature — and it is NOT taken.** Switching a test to its easier form
in a successor register, after the predecessor stopped, is the shape this project distrusts most,
and `W-28`'s rule is that a bar is never relaxed once watched. **The cost is real and is accepted:
two-sided roughly doubles every *p* this register reports.** The pass still additionally requires
a **POSITIVE** point estimate, since a negative drift cannot support buying a call.

Also unchanged and listed so nothing is assumed: the event is `r_t ≤ −2.5 σ_{t−1}` on a **60-session
strictly-prior** scale with a 40-observation minimum, on the **split- and dividend-adjusted** basis
(raw for any strike); entry at the **close of session *t*** with the horizon over *t*+1 … *t*+*h*;
**both** abnormal-return definitions reported and quoting one alone forbidden; halves
**2009–2014 / 2015–2019 with the boundary embargoed**; the economic floor **+0.67pp**; the
permutation bar at **500 draws**; **every critical value LABELLED UNCALIBRATED**; and the
**check quadrant (2020–2026 × half 1) stays CLOSED**, so 2020 and 2022 remain unspent.

1-and-2 together at 10–15%; **those were written when the design had four arms, and this register
keeps them rather than revising them upward on a narrower design** — a narrower design is not a
better hypothesis.

   the residual risk is `K2`–`K5` on a re-run and the arm's own row set.

   because the reversal literature puts the effect at short horizons.
3. **At least one of the two arms flips sign between halves** — 60/35. This is the record's most
   repeated pattern, but with only two arms there are fewer chances for it to appear.
4. **The two abnormal-return definitions disagree in magnitude by more than 2×** — 55/45, on
   `DC-1`'s precedent where they disagreed in **sign**.
5. **Step 1 FAILS** — 65/35. Lower than DIP-CALL's 70/30 only because the design is now powered
   where the literature puts the effect; still the modal outcome, and the binding constraint is
   expected to be the **+0.67pp economic floor** rather than significance.
6. **The full corrected universe and the tier DISAGREE** — 50/50, and a disagreement is a reported
   finding rather than a nuisance (`UNIVERSE-BIAS`, `TIERED-POOL`).
