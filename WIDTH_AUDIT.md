# PART 1 — **W-28 · PETERS–TAYLOR TOTAL Q**, argued properly

## 1.1 The mechanism, stated so that it can be wrong

GAAP **expenses** R&D and most organizational spending rather than capitalizing it. Book equity
therefore understates the true capital base of intangible-heavy firms and understates it
*differentially* — near-zero for a utility, large for a software or pharma name. `book_to_price`
is the ratio of that understated quantity to price. **So the incumbent column is not a noisy
measure of cheapness; it is a measure of cheapness with an error that is CORRELATED WITH
INDUSTRY.**

Peters & Taylor's construction replaces physical capital with **physical + intangible capital**,
where intangible capital is a perpetual inventory on R&D spending (knowledge capital) and on a
fixed share of SG&A (organization capital). Adding that capital back to book equity gives an
**intangible-adjusted book value**, and `book_to_price_adj = (book equity + K_int − already-
capitalized intangibles) / market cap`.

**Three predictions that could each be false, written before anything is measured:**

1. The adjustment is **largest and most systematic in intangible-heavy sectors**, so the
   corrected column should show **less sector-loading** than the incumbent. *(Falsifiable, and
   measurable without touching returns.)*
2. Because measurement error **attenuates** an IC toward zero, de-attenuating the same construct
   should move the column's IC **away from zero in its existing direction**, not reverse it.
   *(If the corrected column's IC flips sign, the "attenuation" story is wrong and the register
   should say so rather than reinterpret.)*
3. The register **explicitly claims NON-orthogonality.** It is not adding information; it is
   claiming the incumbent is measured badly. `MB12`'s pattern — orthogonality has failed as a
   motivation four items deep in its R² band — is therefore **not a hostile prior here, it is the reason this
   register is shaped the way it is.** If the corrected column turns out to be *orthogonal* to
   the incumbent (ρ below ~0.6), **that is a failure of the premise**, not a bonus: it would mean
   the two columns measure different constructs and this is an addition wearing a repair's name.

## 1.3 What the register actually is — a **paired replacement**, and why that is not cosmetic

**Not an addition.** No new column enters the composite; `book_to_price` is **replaced** by
`book_to_price_adj` and the composite keeps 24 z-columns, seven themes, and its shipped weights.

**Scored paired.** Both composites are computed on **the same names and the same 69 dates**, and
every statistic is a **difference series** across those dates, tested with the record's own paired
machinery (Jobson–Korkie/Memmel for Sharpe; the paired HAC t the `S10`/`V2G` rows already use for
alpha).

**And here is the arithmetic that makes the design worth the trial — it has not been written down
anywhere in this record.** Let the composite be linear in its z-columns with `book_to_price`
carrying weight `w` (**1/28** for established names, **1/21** for the rest — §1.4). Then

```
composite_new − composite_old  =  w · (z_adj − z_incumbent)
```

so the paired difference series is the input's change **scaled by w** — and its standard error is
**also** scaled by `w`. **The `w` cancels in the t-statistic.**

> **DILUTION DOES NOT COST POWER IN A PAIRED TEST.** The usual objection to this register — "one
> input of four in one theme of seven is 1/28 of the composite, you will never see it" — is true
> of an *unpaired* comparison against the composite's full standard error, and **false** of the
> paired difference. This is the single strongest argument for the design, and it is the reason
> the register is scored as a difference series rather than as two independent backtests.

**The claim's boundary, stated so it can be checked:** the cancellation is exact only where the
composite is linear in the z-column and the standardisation of the replacement column is computed
the same way as the incumbent's. **`S20` is the proof that this project's standardiser is not a
free parameter** — so the register must use `zscore` **unchanged**, at both layers, or the
cancellation argument does not hold and `S20`'s own rejection applies instead. That is `K5`.

### Coverage — measured on the population the arm will test, and stated before the arm

**`O-1`'s lesson is that a coverage figure measured on one population and applied to another is
worth nothing.** Three coverage numbers are required, and they are different questions:

1. **Crosswalk coverage:** of the **2,531 panel names**, how many resolve to a `gvkey` on the
   **date of each rebalance**, via Sharadar `cusips` → `crsp.stocknames.ncusip` → `permno` →
   CCM → `gvkey`? *`WRDS_UNLOCK_MAP` W-20 already calls this Instrument Zero and says why: a
   90%-coverage link silently truncates every downstream study and looks like a result.*
2. **Field coverage:** among crosswalked name-dates, how many have **non-null `xrd` and `xsga`**
   with enough prior history for a perpetual inventory to have converged (a **burn-in**, not a
   first observation)? Compustat's `xrd` is frequently null for firms that do no R&D — **null and
   zero must be distinguished at declaration**, because treating null as zero is a silent
   imputation that would flow straight into the arm.
3. **Bite coverage — the one that decides the power:** among names with both columns computable,
   on what fraction does the adjustment **move the name's decile**? *A correction that changes a
   number but not an ordering cannot change a portfolio. This is the arm's real n.*

permno span ending 2024-12-31**, and Compustat annual data lags fiscal-year end by construction.

> **The register should expect to lose the panel's most recent quarters — roughly 2025–2026, on
> the order of 5–8 of 69 dates — and it must state the exact usable date list BEFORE the arm,

### Crosswalk risk, named specifically

The panel is **Sharadar-keyed**, and **there is no WRDS product that links Sharadar to
Compustat.** The route is Sharadar `cusips` (already carried in `TICKERS`, and already the anchor

**And this project has been bitten by identity before**, which is why the risk is stated first
rather than as a caveat: the harvest's **45 units over 26 symbols** carrying another company's
history, and `B12`'s alphabetical universe. `ticker_identity.py`'s own docstring records that the

because every symbol returns exactly one permaticker whether or not it was reused. **A crosswalk
audit that reuses that check would report a clean pass on a corrupted join.** The declaration must
name a different check: **`firstpricedate`-anchored, share-class aware, and reported as a
name-date coverage table rather than a single percentage.**

## 1.8 The honest verdict on where W-28 ranks, including where I am overriding the commission's own rule

**The commission's ranking rule is `reopens-a-closed-row > repairs-an-incumbent > new column`.**
By that rule **W-28 is class B**, and class-A entries (`W-1`'s PIT sector map, `W-17`'s index
membership) outrank it.

**I am ranking it first anyway, and here is the argument rather than the assertion.** The class
rule is a **summary of the record's base rates** — a useful prior. The **paired-power advantage is
arithmetic**, not a base rate: at ρ = 0.95 this register sees effects **3.16× smaller** than any
class-A entry scored the ordinary way, on the same 69 dates, for the same one trial. **Where a
base rate and an arithmetic argument disagree about ordering, the arithmetic should win**, and
`SEARCH_DOCTRINE`'s own standing rule says the same thing from the other side: *a direction is
closed by ARITHMETIC or by a MECHANISM, never by a run of nulls alone.*

**Three things this register is not, said plainly:**

* **It is not likely to clear.** The base rate on corrections is **0-for-7 on the alpha gate**,
  `P7` measured that repairing this exact column moves the composite by **+0.05pp**, and `S1`
  measured a mechanism by which the correction could make things **worse**.
* **It is not a defect fix**, and it must not borrow `P7`'s "ships on correctness" standing.
* **It is not cheap in labour** even though it is cheap in trials — the fallback path builds two

**What makes it worth the trial anyway:** it is the **first register in this record that would

collects a power advantage nothing else on the map can collect; **four of its five kills are
free**; and — the part that matters most — **its most likely failure mode teaches something.**
`K4` firing would establish that `book_to_price` is carried for **decorrelation rather than
accuracy**, which no register has ever tested and which would reshape the entire combination axis
that `SEARCH_DOCTRINE` §1.6 left half-closed.

> **A register whose null result is a finding is worth more than a register whose null result is
> a shrug. On that criterion — not on its prior of clearing — W-28 is the best thing width buys.**
