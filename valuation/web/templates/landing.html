📈 Adaptive DCF

Live financials → fair value with bull/base/bear, Monte Carlo, reverse DCF, comps, and a transparent 1–100 score. It adapts to mature, growth, and cash-burning companies.

🔥 Hot-stocks screener

Scans the whole US market and ranks every name on a self-calibrating value · quality · growth · momentum composite, with sector heat. A ranking, not a recommendation — and a coarse one: it says roughly where names stand against each other, not what belongs in your account. {# "roughly" is load-bearing, not hedging.
