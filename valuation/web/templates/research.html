What is deliberately not here

No performance figures. Not one — no return, no t-statistic, no threshold. Where a logged line contained one it reads [figure withheld]. The figures Valquo does publish live on the surfaces that are allowed to carry them, with their caveats attached; repeating them here would turn a page about method into an unaudited second source for numbers, and this project has already shipped that mistake.

No market data, no holdings, no vendor rows. Vendor names appear because they are part of the record.
