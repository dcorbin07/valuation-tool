What is deliberately not here

No performance figures. Not one — no return, no t-statistic, no effect size, and none of the pass marks the log recorded for individual tests. Where a logged line contained one it reads [figure withheld]. The figures Valquo does publish live on the surfaces that are allowed to carry them, with their caveats attached; repeating them here would turn a page about method into an unaudited second source for numbers, and this project has already shipped that mistake.

A few numbers above are not exceptions to that, and it is worth saying why. The bar derived from the trial count is arithmetic on how many things were tried — a count of this project's own decisions. The calibration ceiling and half-width are arithmetic on this project's own written predictions and what became of them. All three contain no market data and say nothing about how the strategy performed; the first is the same for any project that ran the same number of tests. The statistics they are compared against are withheld like every other.

No market data, no holdings, no vendor rows. Vendor names appear because they are part of the record.
