## 1. MECHANISM

The Index serves a large-cap tier, and the cut defining that tier was inherited. Moving it changes
which names are eligible — and because `size` is a scored theme, it also changes the **dispersion**
of the single theme the record says carries the composite's significance.

**ARM: ONE cut, LOWERED, pre-committed.** Lowered rather than raised, for a measured reason given
in §2. The exact threshold is fixed in the register from `P2`'s crowding arithmetic and `D9`'s
coverage finding and is **never moved after reading an outcome** — `W-28`'s closing lesson, which
is that a pre-committed bar may not be relaxed after watching it fail.

## 2. THE RECORD'S EVIDENCE *FOR*

* **`D9` found the names missing from the free scan are mid-cap financials**, so the current cut
  is excluding an identifiable, coherent group rather than a random tail — which is what makes
  this a construction question and not a coverage complaint.
* **The direction is predicted by `U7`, measured.** Inside 187 megacaps *"the other themes are
  compressed and `size` dominates"*, with median market cap rising monotonically across composite
  deciles (**$62.7B → $133.5B**). **So a HIGHER cut compresses the cross-section toward a pure
  size sort.** Lowering the cut widens the dispersion the composite ranks on. That is a mechanism
  for preferring the lower cut and it is the reason this arm has a direction at all.
* **`X3` makes the consequence large.** `size` has the **worst** theme IC (**−0.30**) and carries
  the composite's **entire** statistical significance: adding it last takes alpha **+4.10% →
  +7.17%** and long-short *t* **1.02 → 2.84**. A construction change that widens `size`'s
  dispersion is therefore acting directly on the load-bearing exposure.
* **`X1` says the effect is broad rather than concentrated** — 200 of 200 half-books positive,
  minimum **+0.04382** — which is the shape that tolerates a wider universe.

## 3. THE RECORD'S EVIDENCE *AGAINST* — AND IT IS WHY THIS RANKS LAST

* **`regime_split` measured the edge STRONGEST IN LARGE CAPS.** Lowering the cut adds names from
  the tier where the record measures the edge to be weaker. **That is a direct contradiction of
  §2's mechanism, and both readings are measured** — `U7` says compression hurts at the top,
  `regime_split` says the edge lives at the top. The register must state that the two conflict and
  that it cannot resolve them in advance.
* **Capacity and cost move the wrong way for the served product.** `P1` measured **87 bps at $1M**
  on the top-25 all-cap book against **33.4 bps** on the decile; the all-cap book is the expensive
  one. `B13` is only **PARTIAL** — the categorical screen holds, the liquidity screen does not —
  so a lower cut imports names whose tradability is **not** demonstrated universally.
* **`S10` found the valuation screen preferentially deletes exactly the group `D9` identifies.**
  Flagged-rate **48.88% for Financial Services** and **40.32% for Real Estate** against **15.79%
  for Industrials** — a three-fold spread. So the mid-cap financials a lower cut would admit are
  the names the engine's own bull-case band most often withholds, and the arm risks admitting
  names the product then declines to value.
* **`V6-B`'s gradient runs against the served book**: the survival effect is **−14.287pp** in the
  smallest quintile against **−3.787pp** in megacaps, with its standing caveat that *"the claim is
  strongest exactly where the product is not."* Lowering the cut moves the product toward where
  that claim is strong — which is an argument FOR on risk and says nothing about alpha.
* **`B12` is the cautionary precedent for universe definition.** The 800-name era was an
  **alphabetical A–C slice**, and every figure from it needed re-running. Universe-definition
  changes have already cost this project an entire era of results.

## 4. BAR AND MDE

Gate: the shipped `holdout_compare_panels`, verbatim, both halves, both weightings.

**AND a mandatory `size` control, because without it the arm is uninterpretable.** The arm must be
scored twice — as specified, and with `size` dropped — and **both reported**. If the effect
survives only with `size` present, the arm is a size-dispersion artefact rather than a universe
improvement, which is `U7`'s and `S10`'s shared failure mode (*"the veto vetoes a cap bucket,
which is a property of the underlying"*).

**MDE.** This is **NOT** a clean paired within-panel difference, because the arm **changes the row
set** — and that makes it harder than `IC1`–`IC5`, not easier. A difference between two books on
two different universes cannot use a paired SE at all. The register must state which comparison it
is making and derive the corresponding MDE; equity N **248**, hurdle **3.3206712** (derived). **The
honest expectation is that this is the least resolvable of the six**, and the register should
establish that before running rather than report it afterwards.

## 7. CAN OWNED DATA TEST IT?

**Partly, and the gap matters.** The panel carries `market_cap` on every row, so re-cutting the
tier is free. But the names a lower cut admits must already be IN the 2,531-name panel to be
scorable — **a cut below the panel's own floor is not testable without a universe rebuild**, and
the register must establish, as a free pre-outcome check, that its chosen cut admits only names
the panel already holds. If it does not, the item needs a rebuild and should be declined on cost.
