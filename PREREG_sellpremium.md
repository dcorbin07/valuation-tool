## S11 — `A5`'s CONTROL MUST BE COVERAGE-MATCHED, OR IT MEASURES COVERAGE

`MB1-SEL` is the precedent and it is exact: that register was **VOIDED** because the covered subset
was selected differently in its two arms, and the confound was **twice the size of the effect and
in the same direction**. Here coverage requires a two-sided quote at 0.85–0.95 moneyness and 20–60
DTE, which is a **liquidity** screen, and `O10` measured the liquid part of an options book at
**+5.82%** expectancy against **−3.11%** for the tape-thin part. **So an uncontrolled coverage
difference between the event arm and the random-day control would produce a difference with no
trade in it.**

**Required, before `A5`'s verdict is read: the covered share of candidate days in each arm, and
the differential.** It is a **reported diagnostic** and not a gate — inventing a bar for it after
the fact is what `W-28` forbids — but a differential large enough to account for the effect must
be stated in the same sentence as the verdict.
