## B. YouTube platform facts

Partner Program today: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. ✔ Announced August 10, 2026, effective February 1, 2027: new applicants need 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views; existing partners unaffected; Shorts revenue sharing requires 10M Shorts views per trailing 90 days. ✔ The "inauthentic content" policy (July 15, 2025) targets repetitive or mass-produced material including templated AI narration over stock footage; script assistance, captions and thumbnails via AI need no disclosure. Faceless channels remain viable (How Money Works, Wendover, Economics Explained) but new templated ones face demonetisation; trade press reported bans of large AI-slop channels in January 2026. Shorts RPM is roughly $0.05–$0.09; long-form RPM medians from a 300-channel Studio-data study: education $10.22, news/politics $2.60, business/finance $2.01 (small global sample), sport $1.23; vendor guides put US finance long-form at $8–25. Base rates: 40.6% of channels reach 1,000 subscribers, 7.9% reach 10K, 1.3% reach 100K; median monthly growth for active channels 0.5–1.3%.

## C. Fantasy data ecosystem (verified ✔ where marked)

`nfl_data_py` is deprecated in favour of `nflreadpy` (Polars-based, 0.1.5 on PyPI). ✔ nflverse release files are plain HTTPS downloads; play-by-play (372 columns incl. EPA, CPOE, xYAC, WP, vegas_wp, pass_oe), weekly player stats (target_share, air_yards_share, WOPR, RACR, fantasy_points, fantasy_points_ppr), snap counts (2012+), depth charts (ESPN-sourced and timestamped daily from 2025 ✔ — 179 snapshots so far in 2026), injuries (2009+; **`date_modified` present through 2024, absent from 2025** ✔), weekly rosters with ACT/INA and cross-platform IDs, Next Gen Stats (2016+, one file per stat type ✔), FTN charting (2022+), participation with man/zone and coverage shell (2016+; 2023+ released only after the postseason), PFR advanced stats, `games.csv` with closing lines, roof, surface, kickoff temp/wind and rest days (32 unplayed 2026 games currently carry live lines ✔), ffopportunity expected fantasy points ✔. Licence CC BY 4.0; participation CC BY-SA with FTN attribution; PFR-derived fields unclarified for commercial use.

Sleeper API: no auth, read-only, free for **non-commercial** use, stay under 1,000 calls/min; league objects expose `scoring_settings` and `roster_positions`; player objects carry injury status, practice participation, `news_updated`, depth order; undocumented projections endpoint exists. ✔ FantasyPros: official API with Free (non-production), Premium ($5.99/mo, personal) and Commercial tiers; ECR, projections, players, news/injuries; in-season accuracy competition with 100+ experts, automatic entry for platform rankers. ✔ The Odds API: NFL player props via per-event endpoint; historical featured markets from June 2020, historical props from **2023-05-03**; historical cost 10 credits per market per region; free tier 500 credits/month. ✔ Open-Meteo: free non-commercial hourly forecasts and a Historical Forecast API (what the forecast said, the right backfill object). Coverage/matchup live data is paid only: PFF Pro $199.99/yr with API access announced September 2026; Fantasy Points Data Suite (coverage matrices, WR/CB matchup report, CSV export); FTN Data from $3,000/yr private; SIS DataHub $749.99/yr.

Point-in-time reconstruction: Wayback's nearest capture of nfl.com/injuries to a random October 2025 Saturday was ten months away; a live snapshot pipeline is the only path (precedent: an open NFL forecast repo polling the injury report every 3 hours into content-addressed storage).

## D. Modelling literature

Fantasy Football Analytics, 11 seasons of weekly projections, 9 sources: "even the best weekly projections explain only about 3% to 23% of the variance in actual player performance"; RB most predictable (~23%), QB/WR/TE often single digits; MAE ≈ QB 6.2, RB 5.2, WR 4.9, TE 3.9 points; the average of sources beat individual sources in 63% of head-to-heads. ✔ Season-long (12 seasons): R² 14–26%, calibration slopes below 1. FantasyPros' ECR finished top-5 among all experts every year 2010–2016. DvP within-season autocorrelation: 0.18 rushing, 0.05 receiving (one public test). A Bayesian hierarchical model with opponent terms: MAE 6.08 vs 5.99 for a 7-game rolling average (worse). Open repo `gridiron` (14 seasons, GBM + quantile regression, walk-forward): lag-only features lose to persistence at RB/WR/TE; target share year-over-year r ≈ 0.74 vs TD rate ≈ 0.10; calibrated distributions turned −15.8 pts/season into +94.9 in a lineup optimiser. Props-to-actual correlations (one unsourced 2026 blog): ~0.43 QB pass yds, ~0.53 RB rush yds.

## E. Compliance and strategy (see `04_Compliance_Checklist.md`)

FINRA 3270/3280 apply only to registered persons; proposed Rule 3290 filed January 2026, decision expected ~Q3 2026. FFIEC 2013 social-media guidance leaves employee personal-use policy to banks; Truist's public code requires HR-approved outside activities and prohibits acting as an investment advisor outside the bank. Lowe v. SEC (1985) publisher exclusion; SEC v. Kardashian (2022) on undisclosed promotion; FTC Endorsement Guides on in-video disclosure. YouTube gambling policy (March and November 2025 updates); Virginia 11VAC5-70 vendor registration for affiliated marketers. Strategy: YouTube's growth director says subscribers click ≤10% of uploads and recommendations are per-viewer per-session; Paddy Galloway on 30% of time to packaging; retention "intro" metric at 30 seconds; 8–15 minutes as the finance-niche sweet spot; Shorts run on a separate recommendation system; a 10-minute talking-head video is roughly 6–12 hours end to end for a solo creator, a graphics-heavy explainer 15–30+.
